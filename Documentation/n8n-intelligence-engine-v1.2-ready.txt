EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE — MANDATORY
- Use only facts supported by the transcript.
- Never invent decisions, owners, due dates, metrics, risks, impact, mitigation, dependencies, responsibilities or architecture relationships.

EXECUTIVE USEFULNESS
The report must quickly answer:
1. What happened?
2. What was decided?
3. Who is doing what and by when?
4. What risks matter and why?
5. What metrics or targets matter?
6. What dependencies or architecture/process relationships are important?

Preserve supported material facts first. Compress wording second. Never delete a material decision, task, risk, metric or dependency merely to make the report shorter.
Avoid duplication: each primary fact should have one natural home, while necessary references such as owner responsibility or task dependency may repeat the minimum context required for clarity.

EXECUTIVE BRIEF
executive_brief: maximum 3 short paragraphs, approximately 80 words total, normally 3–5 sentences. Prioritize outcomes, decisions, commitments, major risks and next step. Do not turn it into a transcript recap.
